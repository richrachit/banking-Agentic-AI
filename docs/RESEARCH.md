# Design and Regulatory Notes

The source documents require deterministic statutory rules and human approval for regulated actions. The implementation therefore uses explainable rules for dormancy and financial analysis rather than autonomous LLM decisions.

India DEA, UDGAM, Account Aggregator, KYC/V-CIP, DPDP and tax integrations require current bank legal/compliance confirmation and authoritative technical specifications. Configuration and local simulated acknowledgements must not be interpreted as certification or legal advice.

Before production, verify effective-dated RBI directions, DEA interest/remittance/refund treatment, AA consent/data-use obligations, regulator file formats, privacy/residency, records retention and model-risk expectations with the accountable bank functions.
